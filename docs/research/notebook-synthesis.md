## Professional mechanisms

Query `a275a89ac9a6` selected the professional documentation and careers page. Its raw result is `.artifacts/research/notebook-professional-synthesis-20261018.json`. The existing [journey reuse record](journeys-reuse.md) supplies the source comparison and adopted decisions.

[Frappe leave applications](https://docs.frappe.io/hr/leave-application) connect the employee's dates, balance, responsible approver and later decision. JanaMen adopts that information relationship. It keeps its own server states and explicit submission command. Frappe's payroll lock, allocation periods, half days and email delivery are not JanaMen capabilities or new requirements.

[Moodle activity completion](https://docs.moodle.org/501/en/Activity_completion) distinguishes completion conditions from merely opening material. [Moodle quiz documentation](https://docs.moodle.org/501/en/Quiz_activity) distinguishes attempts, submission and retained results. JanaMen exposes prerequisites and saved attempts through its existing APIs. The synthesis also proposed intermediate answer autosave and a separate final-summary screen. Neither is adopted or claimed without a matching contract.

The [Jana Post careers page](https://www.janapost.kz/ru/v2/careers) supports a distributed logistics and technology context. Its current vacancies do not establish private HR policies, integration capability or economic benefit. The synthesis's vacancy count is not used.

## Decisions changed

The defense follows a complete employee-to-manager-to-HR result before showing secondary modules because the official journey criterion has the largest weight. Completion, failed attempts and archived history remain separate because the Moodle mechanisms distinguish them. Frappe-specific rules and NotebookLM's extra suggestions do not become fabricated JanaMen UI. Notebook synthesis supports research; source inspection and actual browser results determine acceptance.
