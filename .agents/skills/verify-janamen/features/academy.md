# Learning and publication

## Sub-features

Thirty-course catalog; own assignments; material progress; quiz; attempt history; HR publication and assignment.

## How to get to it (user POV)

Use /academy as learner and /hr/courses or /hr/assignments as HR.

## Driving it with e2e

Write tests/web/academy.e2e.ts from observed labels. Confirm the exact published revision before each attempt.

## Proof

A failed attempt can precede a successful one. Progress survives reopen. New publication and archive preserve earlier history. Learner responses contain no answer key.

## Charters

`learning-recovery|chromium|default|Using --session employee alone, inspect a failed quiz then return to completed material and verify preserved progress.`

## Gotchas

No self-enrollment, invented timer or client grading. Required material and pass result are separate requirements.
